## Card <-> city links (feat/card-city-links)

Plan: [[test-plan-2026-07-27-card-city-links]].

- New [[roadGeometry]] module (lib/seed/roadGeometry.ts): district runs along a
  polyline (entry order + length inside) and a crossing search that grids the
  target road's segments and streams the other 106k past it. Highway 9: 13
  districts / 52 crossings, 0.3ms + 8ms, memoised per road.
- Road cards: Districts became a VERTICAL list below the stats, ordered along
  the road with the entry km mark; highways gained a Crossings list (route
  order, tier badged) - the seam where offramps will attach; Length added,
  zero-count rows hidden. Highways carry no addresses at all, which is why
  their cards were empty.
- Building rows on street cards adopted the directory's format; AddrNum moved
  into EntityColumns so the directory and the cards share one implementation.
- Hover-to-highlight generalised across cards ([[useCityHover]]): a row
  highlights what clicking it would open. New store channels hoverRoadId +
  hoverBuildingId (white polyline / white cage, brighter than the selection
  cage); districts and tenant units reuse the existing ones. Cleared on unmount
  AND on column change (a click can reuse the card component).
- Occupant lists sort top floor first (regionFor*.floorStart desc) so hovering
  down the list walks down the building.
- Clicking the focused building's SHELL (no unit behind the ray) now returns
  the drill to that building's card - the any-side unit picking had made a
  shell click a no-op.
