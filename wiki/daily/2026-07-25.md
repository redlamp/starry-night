## Root causes found

- **Skyline band dead zone (1.1)**: the R pose is DEFAULT_INTENT, which rests
  at ~1.06 deg looking UP - inside the fixed 1.0-1.5 deg hysteresis pair the
  2026-07-19 plan sized against DEFAULT_ORBIT's 2 deg. Crop-follow scaling
  (horizontal-only, #56) shrinks the angle further. Once latched under 1 deg,
  the pose could never release at rest -> "R makes LMB pedestal".
- **Mid-drag seams (2.**)**: perspective pan changes elevation silently when
  the focal pins at the disc rim and the eye backs out - the live latch then
  flipped truck <-> pedestal semantics mid-gesture.
- **Compass spins (3.*)**: the needle bearing was a screen-space projection;
  both atan2 components shrink with sin(elevation), so pose noise near
  skyline amplified into full spins. Replaced with a ground-plane bearing
  (right vs forward+up horizontals) - stable everywhere, still tracks the
  top-down park's roll.
