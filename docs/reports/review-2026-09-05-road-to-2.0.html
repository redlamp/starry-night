Project review

Road to Starry Night 2.0

Connecting to the shared store…

Version 1 shipped in June and the project kept building past its own spec for two months. The features of a second generation are in the tree. The paperwork, the gates, and the one milestone that was always half-open are not.

Where we are

Call it 1.8

The PRD's four v1 milestones are done and mostly exceeded. Since then the city went metro-scale with tensor-field roads and suburbs, gained residents with families and story hooks, air and road traffic, a Mac-Plus intro, click-to-inspect, and a continuous camera. That is a second product, not a point release. It is not 2.0 yet because nothing says what 2.0 is, the window-dynamics milestone is still "infrastructure ready, not user-facing" three months on, and the one playtest's deepest findings were never acted on.

The tags are dates, the package says 0.1.0, and nothing in the product states a version. The number above is a judgment, and the last section proposes a scheme so the next one is not.

Direction from the read-through (09-05): stay in 1.x releases for now. 2.0 is the plan, not the next tag. The PRD is a stale instrument; the Paper phase below offers a replacement rather than a rewrite.

What was missed

Things the record says are open, in flight, or done that reality disagrees with.

Tier 1 (camera feel, skyline band) shipped within a day. The deeper findings, an interaction-mode split between spatial toy and directory browse, information hierarchy, and engagement, were never filed as issues. The July 27 daily lists them as unfiled. Six weeks later nothing moved. This is the single most substantive open thread in the project.

What to reconsider

Decisions that are yours. Each has my recommendation. Tick the ones you agree with and add a note where you disagree or want a different shape. Every finding, phase, and table row on this page takes a mark and a note too. All of it saves to the page's shared store, and Claude reads it back from the session.

The fable branch is 35 commits behind dev, 0 ahead, and has had no commits since July 27. It exists for attribution, and the merge history already preserves that whether or not new work routes through it. Now that Fable is the everyday model, the detour costs a merge per feature and a rule that other sessions keep breaking. Recommend: freeze fable as history, route everything feat/* off dev. This contradicts a standing project rule, so it is your call, not mine.

Andy's deepest finding. The app currently serves both from one surface. Recommend: a decision note before any more camera or card work, then a second playtest to check it.

The most recent work is face-generation research (#100). The persona-presence plan still has tenant highlighting unverified in-app and storefront clustering deferred. Portraits are a 2.x feature with real research momentum. Recommend: finish presence first. Keep the probe, park #100 until 2.0 tags.

StarryNightV2Model is 1,205 lines beside V3's 2,229. The July 27 daily flags "v2 camera model on old mapping" as open. Recommend: retire V2 once #98 skyline ergonomics lands, and split V3 while you are in there.

Suburbs shipped. The highway-corridor stages of plan-metro-suburbs-highways were never built and #13 closed on a rejected spike. Recommend: mark the plan partly superseded and drop corridors unless bus routes (#91) need them.

There are no test files and no runner. There are 47 scripts, of which about a dozen are real assertion gates (gate1 20/20, personaCheck 17/17, cityGolden, and friends). Recommend: a single "bun run test" that runs the gate scripts, wired into CI. No vitest migration for 2.0.

feature/road-reveal has 15 unmerged commits from June; feat/figma has 7 that belong to another repo; two worktree-agent branches are fully merged. Recommend: delete figma and the worktree branches, and either land or archive-tag road-reveal.

Hardware-blocked since July. Recommend: decide from the device-tier table without the low-spec device: default to the tier below what tier detection picks on coarse pointers, and revisit when hardware is available.

Versioning

Keep the date tags. They are the ship log and the one-per-day rule works. Add a milestone number that the product itself states.

  • package.json carries semver and bumps only at milestones: 1.8.0 at the end of the sweep, 2.0.0 at the end of phase 4. Point releases between are optional.
  • Tags stay vYYYY.MM.DD and mark deploys. A milestone tag (v2.0.0) is added alongside the date tag on the day it ships.
  • The seed chip shows the version next to the seed, so a shared screenshot says which city and which build.
  • A full number means a new PRD. 3.0 gets written down before it gets built.
